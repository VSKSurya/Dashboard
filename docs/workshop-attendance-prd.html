Workshop Attendance App

Draft 0113 October 2026Web app, initial scope

A practical tool for workshop organizers to maintain student rosters, record who attended each session, track participation time, and produce useful attendance summaries.

01Overview

The app supports the operational attendance needs of student workshops. An administrator maintains the roster and sessions; an instructor records attendance for a specific session and date; authorized users review history, analytics, and downloadable reports.

Product direction

Make it quick and unambiguous to complete a session’s attendance, while preserving a reliable history that can be searched and reported later.

02Problem and goals

Problem

Organizers need a dependable record of which students joined each workshop. Paper lists and scattered spreadsheets make it difficult to keep student records current, identify missing attendance, understand participation patterns, and prepare reports.

Goals

  • Manage a current list of workshop students.
  • Record present or absent status for each student for each session.
  • Make session dates and attendance completion visible in a calendar.
  • Capture time spent when the workshop process supports it.
  • Summarize and export attendance data for follow-up and reporting.

03Users and scope

Administrator

Maintains students and workshop sessions, reviews attendance, and creates reports.

Instructor

Opens a session roster, marks attendance, and reviews that session’s saved record.

MVP scope

  • Student roster with add, edit, deactivate, search, and unique student identifier.
  • Workshop/session and date selection.
  • Attendance states of present, absent, and unmarked.
  • Save and revise attendance, with a visible saved/completion state.
  • Calendar view of scheduled sessions and attendance completion.
  • Student and session attendance history, basic summaries, and CSV export.

Later or optional scope

  • Check-in/check-out based time tracking, if the workshop needs precise durations.
  • Notifications, student self-service, QR or location check-in, integrations, and complex multi-organization permissions.

Behavior and usability

  • Unmarked must remain distinct from Absent so incomplete records are not counted as absences.
  • Show a clear save confirmation and warn before leaving with unsaved attendance changes.
  • Prevent duplicate attendance entries for the same student and session.
  • Make roster search and attendance controls usable by keyboard and touch.
  • Use clear empty states when no students, sessions, or records exist.

05Key workflows

Record session attendance

Manage a student

  1. Administrator opens the student list and chooses Add student.
  2. Administrator enters the required identity fields and saves.
  3. The student appears in the roster for eligible workshop sessions.
  4. If the student leaves, the administrator deactivates the record; historical attendance remains reportable.

Review a report

  1. User selects a date range and optional workshop or student filters.
  2. App displays matching attendance and summary metrics.
  3. User exports the filtered rows as CSV.

06Data and business rules

RecordMinimum fields
StudentStudent ID, name, active status, created/updated timestamps.
Workshop sessionSession ID, workshop/title, date and optional start/end time, status.
AttendanceStudent ID, session ID, status, optional duration/check-in data, updated by, updated timestamp.
  • One attendance record exists per student per session.
  • Only students on that session’s roster are included in its attendance checklist.
  • Attendance status is one of Unmarked, Present, or Absent.
  • Attendance completion is based on every roster student receiving a final status; unmarked students remain outstanding.
  • Student deactivation does not delete attendance history.
  • Duration is optional until the time-tracking method and policy are confirmed.

07Success measures and quality

Product measures

  • Instructors can complete a session’s roster without assistance.
  • Attendance records can be located by student, workshop, and date.
  • Administrators can export a filtered report successfully.
  • Sessions with missing/unmarked entries are visible and distinguishable from complete sessions.

Quality requirements

  • Responsive layouts support desktop and tablet use, with touch-friendly controls.
  • Use semantic headings, labeled controls, keyboard focus indicators, and readable contrast.
  • Protect student information through authenticated access and role-appropriate permissions before production use.
  • Keep a reliable change timestamp and user attribution for attendance edits.

08Open questions and assumptions
