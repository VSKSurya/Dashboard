To ensure the safety of your account and personal data, your password must meet the following security criteria:

  • Minimum 8 characters.
  • At least one uppercase letter (A–Z).
  • At least one lowercase letter (a–z).
  • At least one number (0–9).
  • At least one special character, such as ! @ # $ %.
  • No spaces.
  • Different from your previous password.

Avoid your name, username, mobile number, email address or date of birth.
