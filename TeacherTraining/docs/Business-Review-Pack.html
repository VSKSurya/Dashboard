Requirements summary

Central project-owned teacher/student training management covers master trainers/availability/holidays/resources/forms; project selection from Odoo; project training plans with day activities; Excel-led batches with school-derived location, capacity, trainers and holiday checks; Excel/open registration with OTP and final trainer approval; attendance; completion/certification; six role dashboards and nine MIS reports. Role/project scope, masking and auditing apply throughout.

Confirmed requirements are explicit statements in §§1–10,12 and consolidated §28. Suggested statuses, completion indicators, dashboard layouts, recommended KPIs/reports and conditional “may” requirements remain recommendations pending sign-off, even where the prototype demonstrates them.

Conflict register: Q3 says date ordering/boundaries are not stated, while §5 requires start strictly before end and §7 requires dates within project. Explicit statements are enforced in review and the conflict remains open. §§2/5 give broad role abilities while Q1 asks for exact grants. §10 permits attendance changes yet Q29 asks whether corrections are allowed: demo immediate audited corrections are provisional. §9 conditional resource propagation is not automatic confirmation. Q14 retains encryption/masking/clear-certificate tension. No earlier chat length, past-date, noon cutoff, offline attendance or assignment-upload rule is imported.

Lifecycle review

Plan and batch creation require project links; registration requires capacity; approval precedes final closure. Registration → Approved occurs only through the assigned trainer. Approved → Training Started is derived from recorded attendance. Training Completed is evaluated only with provisional configured attendance/days/forms; Assessment Completed records actual submissions but does not imply passing. Certified is a demo preview outcome only after configured eligibility. Cancelled, Dropped/Inactive, rejection, reopening and retrospective schedule changes require business decisions; no production transitions are invented. Date-derived Upcoming is a dashboard category, distinct from suggested Not Started.

Demo KPI and report definitions
